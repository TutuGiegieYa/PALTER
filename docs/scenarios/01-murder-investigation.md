# Murder Investigation Scenario

Status: Murder investigation and optional voluntary opening implemented.

Last updated: 2026-11-06.

## Current Runtime

The optional opening implements every local opening rule below, including all-human fallback and later leave/inspect/touch choices. Three fixed causal families cover displaced clock readings, recorded alibis and object substitution. New case rules version 3 gives the latter two distinct custody and identity-transfer proof structures, with an innocent initial registrant, a privately held counterfoil and an independently searchable archive. Initial registration is a misleading lead, not a mandatory proof premise once actual custody is established. Original/copy equivalents earn one key-evidence contribution, and supporting victory requires a correct, partly reasoned accusation as well as two first disclosures. Saved older cases keep their original assets and objectives. Two identity proof routes and protected backups permit reconstruction after some removable evidence is handled. Primary and conflicting protect/expose objectives are scored separately. The one-category disruption allowance applies per actor per 1,440 physical clock units; categories are document or trace, and same-category retries consume real time. No fixed act count or forced deadline ends play.

See [runtime and verification](../17-complete-playable-runtime.md). Tests exercise both successful effects and failed/partial goals. The setting and broader ending alternatives below are retained as design material; this section defines the implemented policy.

## Local Rules from the User's Example

- Most participants have a history with the designated target.
- Participants can choose whether to kill the target.
- This opening produces one victim and exactly one actual killer.
- Randomize arrival while giving willing humans priority over willing AI characters.
- If nobody volunteers and AI characters are present, assign one AI character to kill.
- If nobody volunteers and no AI character is present, randomly assign a human.
- The first assigned killer completes the killing and leaves. Later arrivals find the victim already dead.
- Later characters may inspect or touch the scene, leave accidental traces, and conceal their visits for personal reasons.

These policies apply to this particular example, not all mystery stories or all PALTER games. Discovering a body does not itself reveal the killer.

## Setup and Objectives Proposal

The user has confirmed progressive truth deduction as the primary experience.
The implemented bounded slice is [Demo 02](../16-demo-02-deduction.md): an
already-occurred murder, a fixed coherent truth, discoverable evidence and
attributed testimony, and a reasoned final accusation. The human should be able
to revise an initial interpretation by connecting facts. Evidence-based success
is distinct from correctly guessing a name or retrieving an item. Its investigation-after-death
opening does not implement or supersede the voluntary-killing example above.

Provide a shared location, private connections to the target, individual past experiences, and reasons to conceal parts of the truth.

Possible objectives include reconstructing the case, avoiding exposure, protecting someone, explaining a suspicious visit, or preserving a separate secret. An innocent character can help a suspect without knowing the suspect's actual role.

## Events and Action Opportunities

A public gathering, temporary access change, inspection, unexpected visitor, journey between areas, or power failure can create opportunities for discovery, guarding, movement, and evidence handling. A nighttime window is one optional setting.

The user's daily budget example permits attempts against at most one evidence category per day. Actor scope, category definitions, retries, and adoption in a concrete story remain open.

## Ending Proposals

- Correct reconstruction accepted by the group.
- Truth understood privately but not demonstrated.
- Wrong accusation or a misleading consensus.
- Deliberate collective concealment or a protective sacrifice.
- Relevant removable evidence gone while earlier memories or logical clues may remain.
- Voluntary unresolved closure, with different personal and relationship outcomes. The current investigation has no prescribed act count or deadline.

Hidden history remains consistent. A lack of recognized clues does not alone trigger an ending; stopping rules must be defined.

## Broader Design Questions

Fallback-role disclosure, reasons for visits and silence, action timing, evidence budgets, accusation rules, and post-game disclosure.
