# Council and Succession Scenario

Status: Distinct votes, resource pledges, transfers and coalition outcome implemented.

Last updated: 2026-11-05.

## Current Runtime

Each of four characters has one revisable vote and starts with two resource units. At least three votes for one candidate create stable authorization. A promise and an actual resource transfer are separate state changes; only matching transfer fulfills a pledge. The primary goal requires voting for the established leader, and the personal goal requires fulfilling a recorded pledge. Private speech cannot vote or transfer on behalf of another character.

See [runtime and verification](../17-complete-playable-runtime.md). Tests exercise both successful effects and failed/partial goals. The setting and broader ending alternatives below are retained as design material; this section defines the implemented policy.

## Premise and Initial Information

A council must choose leadership or allocate authority before an external deadline. Participants represent overlapping interests, private obligations, and competing interpretations of legitimacy.

Initial memories describe earlier bargains, disputes, and promises. Relationships can cut across factions, allowing a participant to protect someone outside their nominal side.

## Objectives and Actions

Objectives may include securing influence, preserving stability, honoring a commitment, protecting a dependent group, or preventing a rival agreement.

Characters can make proposals, inspect authorized records, negotiate privately after public invitation and agreement, pledge resources, withdraw support, or attend a public hearing.

## Events and Constraints

A delegation arrives, a deadline changes under established conditions, a claim is publicly challenged, or access to a relevant record opens. These events can occur during hearings, travel, or other scene opportunities.

Commitments, resource ownership, and voting or agreement rules must be explicit. A character's private choice is not automatically known to the council.

## Ending Proposals

A durable coalition; a dominant faction with unstable support; power sharing; a principled withdrawal; deadlock; or a formally successful agreement that breaks important personal commitments.

## Broader Design Questions

Decision procedure, resources, binding promises, timing, legitimacy criteria, and disclosure of private agreements.
