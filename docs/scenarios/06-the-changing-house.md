# The Changing House Scenario

Status: Distinct fixed environmental switch mechanism, keepsakes and escape outcomes implemented.

Last updated: 2026-11-05.

## Current Runtime

The initial hidden mechanism is an ordinary two-switch circuit: the exit is accessible exactly when the switches differ. The circuit explanation is fixed before play. Available records explain its rule; switching affects real access, and each character independently chooses departure. Characters can retrieve their private keepsakes before leaving. Full escape and personal recovery are evaluated separately. Broader supernatural and perceptual variants below remain optional setting proposals.

See [runtime and verification](../17-complete-playable-runtime.md). Tests exercise both successful effects and failed/partial goals. The setting and broader ending alternatives below are retained as design material; this section defines the implemented policy.


Category proposal: Horror and the Uncanny.

References: [Catalog](README.md), [Memory and state](../02-memory-state-evidence.md), [Endings](../06-goals-and-endings.md).

## Premise and Initial Information

Visitors enter a place where sound, access, and appearances behave according to hidden but consistent rules. Characters bring different prior experiences and reasons to stay or leave.

Whether the explanation is ordinary, supernatural, or a specified combination belongs to the scenario's hidden setup. It must not be rewritten midway to match guesses.

## Objectives and Actions

Characters may seek an explanation, a missing person or object, a safe route, protection of a companion, or completion of a prior promise.

Actions include comparing observations, testing permitted environmental rules, exploring together or separately, preserving shared reference points, and deciding whether to trust an account.

## Events and Constraints

A room changes under established conditions, a sound draws attention, a temporary route appears, or a known object returns through an actual scenario mechanism. Events can occur in daylight, during a gathering, or at another appropriate moment.

Uncertain perception must have defined causes and observation limits. The game retains a consistent actual history even when characters misunderstand it.

## Ending Proposals

An explanation established; escape with unresolved questions; recovery of the objective at a relationship cost; a mistaken interpretation; or a failed departure under the scenario's actual rules.

## Broader Design Questions

Hidden explanation, intensity and tone, perception rules, route conditions, and what the introduction can safely describe.
