# The Sealed Archive Scenario

Status: Distinct fixed dial mechanism, archive custody and partial recovery implemented.

Last updated: 2026-11-05.

## Current Runtime

Two dials each have three possible positions. Their correct values are fixed at setup and disclosed in separate original records. Only the matching pair opens the inner cabinet; opening alone recovers nothing. The group must physically collect at least two of three records. Each role needs an active contribution and must personally retain one recovered record for complete success. Partial recovery remains visible in the ending. No special phrase or model assertion bypasses the mechanism.

See [runtime and verification](../17-complete-playable-runtime.md). Tests exercise both successful effects and failed/partial goals. The setting and broader ending alternatives below are retained as design material; this section defines the implemented policy.

## Premise and Initial Information

A group gains limited access to an archive or research site containing mechanisms and fragmented records. Each character holds different knowledge and interests in what is recovered.

Initial relationships include prior cooperation, academic rivalry, obligations, and trust in different sources.

## Objectives and Actions

Characters can seek a shared discovery, recovery of particular records, protection of private material, or validation of an interpretation.

Actions include examining permitted mechanisms, combining known facts, exchanging observations, allocating access time, preserving material, and choosing which route or question to pursue.

## Events and Constraints

Access opens for a limited period, an environmental condition changes a mechanism, a record becomes reachable, or another group requests cooperation.

Logical connections can remain unrecognized even after component facts are found. A valid solution should follow the world's rules rather than require a particular phrase or an unexplained answer item.

## Ending Proposals

Complete recovery and sound interpretation; a correct mechanism solution with partial historical understanding; valuable recovery under a mistaken interpretation; shared knowledge with broken trust; or departure with only part of the objective completed.

## Broader Design Questions

Mechanism rules, clue dependencies, access budgets, sharing incentives, and criteria for partial success.
