# Reunion and Family Secrets Scenario

Status: Distinct bilateral commitments, disclosure and reconciliation runtime implemented.

Last updated: 2026-11-05.

## Current Runtime

Initial personas, secrets and relationship histories are model-authored and fixed. Characters inspect/share records, propose a commitment, accept one addressed to themselves, or withdraw a previously agreed commitment. Only explicit agreement establishes a bond. Two agreed commitments establish the shared outcome. Personal reconciliation additionally requires an actual record contribution and an agreed bond involving that role. Competing protect/expose goals and directional trust/affinity retain consequences.

See [runtime and verification](../17-complete-playable-runtime.md). Tests exercise both successful effects and failed/partial goals. The setting and broader ending alternatives below are retained as design material; this section defines the implemented policy.

## Premise and Initial Information

A reunion brings together people connected by promises, old misunderstandings, and a decision about their shared future. There need not be a crime or designated offender.

Initial memories differ in perspective and completeness. Initial relationships include affection, resentment, debts, dependence, and knowledge of private connections.

## Objectives and Actions

Characters may seek reconciliation, an explanation, independence, fulfillment of a promise, or protection of someone else's privacy.

Actions include examining available keepsakes or records, arranging an agreed private conversation, making or withdrawing a commitment, sharing an account, and deciding whether to attend a joint event.

## Events and Constraints

An unexpected visitor, delayed letter, changed travel plan, ceremony, or newly accessible record creates opportunities outside ordinary dialogue.

Past facts remain stable. Revealing a connection changes who knows it; changing affection does not erase shared history. Disclosures and relationship changes respect individual knowledge.

## Ending Proposals

Reconciliation with disclosure; affection retained despite disagreement; an honest separation; apparent harmony built on a secret; a misunderstanding left unresolved; or personal independence with a lasting relationship cost.

## Broader Design Questions

Public occasion, relationship dimensions, disclosure boundaries, commitments, and how human players express changing attitudes.
