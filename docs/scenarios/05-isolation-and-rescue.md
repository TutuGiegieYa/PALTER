# Isolation and Rescue Scenario

Status: Distinct supplies, timed repair, route clearance and individual evacuation implemented.

Last updated: 2026-11-05.

## Current Runtime

The group starts with four battery and four supply units. Communications repair at the worksite consumes two batteries; clearing the rear route consumes two supplies. Each occupies two shared clock units and can be interrupted without refunding consumed supplies. AI-selected resupply is an eligible world event. A character may evacuate from the rear location only after both systems work; every character chooses independently. Full rescue requires all four departures, while each personal goal additionally requires carrying that character's keepsake. Arbitrary tarpaulin assembly remains a design illustration, not a claimed implemented effect.

See [runtime and verification](../17-complete-playable-runtime.md). Tests exercise both successful effects and failed/partial goals. The setting and broader ending alternatives below are retained as design material; this section defines the implemented policy.

## Premise and Initial Information

A remote station or expedition loses reliable access to outside assistance. The group must manage limited in-game resources and investigate possible routes while circumstances change.

Initial memories include personal responsibilities, local knowledge, prior promises, and differing accounts of available resources. Relationships affect whose needs each participant prioritizes.

## Objectives and Actions

Characters may seek group evacuation, rescue of particular people, recovery of an important item, or completion of an obligation.

Actions include allocating fictional resources, investigating permitted routes, guarding supplies, restoring scenario facilities, choosing who accompanies an expedition, or waiting for more information.

### Free-Action Illustration: Protecting Supplies

Under the proposed [shared action policy](../04-world-events-and-actions.md), a character can propose stretching an available tarpaulin between existing supports to protect supplies from rain, even if the scenario menu does not list that exact sentence.

Compose supported movement, object handling, and construction operations. Check actual materials, access, time, and relevant character capability; resolve effects on the supplies under the scenario's weather conditions. Do not invent missing materials or turn a temporary cover into a guaranteed solution to unrelated evacuation goals. If assistance is needed, another participant must decide whether to help.

Ordinary assembly under adequate conditions need not receive an arbitrary failure roll. Established wind or material limitations may support uncertainty, interruption, or partial protection. Record the actual outcome and each character's observations without forcing additional damage to preserve a predetermined story.

## Events and Constraints

Weather changes, a route becomes inaccessible, a visitor arrives, communication is restored temporarily, or transport capacity changes under scenario conditions.

Resources and travel conditions must have consistent effects. Events create meaningful choices; their consequences follow the actual group response.

## Ending Proposals

Full rescue; partial rescue; survival with an abandoned secondary objective; a voluntary costly choice; escape accompanied by broken trust; or failure to coordinate before a defined deadline.

## Broader Design Questions

Time model, resource budgets, route discovery, shared decision procedures, and private objectives.
